# CRITICAL PRIORITY: IELTS ACADEMIC READING 7.5 MODULE

Reading is one of the highest-priority parts of this application.

Do NOT build the Reading section as a simple passage + multiple-choice quiz.

Build it as a serious IELTS Academic Reading training system designed to prepare a learner for approximately Band 7.5 performance.

The system must provide:

1. Question-type-specific training
2. Progressive difficulty
3. Exam-realistic passages
4. Exam-realistic distractors
5. Detailed post-answer explanations
6. Exact evidence highlighting
7. Keyword/paraphrase analysis
8. Mistake classification
9. Weakness tracking
10. Timed full Reading tests
11. Reading history and analytics
12. Adaptive recommendations

---

# A. OFFICIAL-STYLE TEST STRUCTURE

Full Reading mock tests should follow the IELTS Academic structure:

* 60 minutes
* 3 passages
* 40 questions total
* no extra answer-transfer time
* increasing difficulty from Passage 1 to Passage 3
* approximately 2,150–2,750 words total across the three passages

Passage style should resemble serious non-specialist material drawn conceptually from:

* science
* history
* technology
* psychology
* archaeology
* environment
* education
* medicine/health
* economics
* transportation
* engineering
* sociology
* biology
* climate
* architecture
* business
* agriculture
* language
* urban planning
* innovation

At least some Passage 3 texts should contain substantial argument, competing viewpoints, inference, or complex logical structure.

# B. READING TRAINING ARCHITECTURE

Divide question-specific practice into SEVEN core families.

Each family must eventually contain:

25 practice passage sets

Therefore:

7 × 25 = 175 targeted Reading practice sets.

Do NOT generate all 175 low-quality exercises in one uncontrolled AI batch.

Create the architecture first.

Content should be stored as structured seed data.

Then populate it progressively while validating answer quality.

Level 2:

Level 3:

Level 4:

Level 5:
subtle NOT GIVEN/inference traps

Teach the critical distinction:

TRUE:

FALSE:
the passage contradicts the statement

NOT GIVEN:
the passage does not provide enough information to decide

# AJ. CONTENT VALIDATION SYSTEM

This is extremely important.

Before a passage/question is considered production-ready, validate:

1. Correct answer is supported by passage.
2. Evidence location exists.
3. No other answer is equally defensible.
4. NOT GIVEN questions genuinely lack required information.
5. FALSE/NO questions contain contradiction.
6. Word limit is compatible with answer.
7. Matching headings have one clear best answer.
8. Multiple-choice distractors are plausible but wrong.
9. Grammar of completion answer works.
10. Evidence and explanation are consistent.
11. Passage difficulty matches its assigned level.
12. Questions do not accidentally reveal answers to later questions.

Create validation tests where feasible.
