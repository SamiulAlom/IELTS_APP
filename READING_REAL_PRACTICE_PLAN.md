# 13. MATCHING HEADINGS REVIEW EXAMPLE

After submission:

Question 3

Status:
Incorrect

Your Answer:
v

Correct Answer:
ii

Paragraph:
C

KEY LINE:

"The introduction of automated machinery dramatically reduced the need for manual labour."

Question Idea:

Effect of automation on workers

Passage Idea:

Reduced demand for manual labour

Paraphrase:

workers became unnecessary
→
reduced need for manual labour

Why Your Answer Was Wrong:

You selected a heading based on a supporting example near the beginning of the paragraph rather than identifying the paragraph's overall purpose.

Trap:

DETAIL VS MAIN IDEA

Tip:

For Matching Headings, summarize the entire paragraph in a few words before checking the list of headings.

---

# 14. T/F/NG REVIEW EXAMPLE

Statement:

The scientists expected the experiment to fail.

Your Answer:

FALSE

Correct Answer:

NOT GIVEN

Evidence:

The passage describes the results of the experiment but never states what the scientists expected beforehand.

Why FALSE Is Wrong:

FALSE requires the passage to state the opposite.

No opposite expectation is given.

Trap:

FALSE VS NOT GIVEN

Lesson:

Do not decide based on what seems logical. Decide only from information explicitly present in the text.
